[Inyerix](https://www.instagram.com/inyerix) was an Instagram account I operated between 2015-2018, which now serves as a record of the start to my digital art and design journey.

Early posts relied on a several photo-editing apps, while later works are predominantly created in Photoshop, with images from [Unsplash](https://www.unsplash.com).
